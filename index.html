About Us

The Maine State SciTech Policy Fellowship places scientists and technical experts directly within state offices and partner organizations. Hosted in partnership with Maine Sea Grant, the program equips fellows to translate peer-reviewed science into actionable state policy while building lasting networks across Maine's government and research institutions.

Core Research & Policy Areas

Coastal Science

Working waterfront resilience, marine ecosystems, sea-level rise adaptation, and fisheries management.

Energy

Offshore wind development, grid modernization, clean energy transitions, and rural energy access.

Housing

Sustainable coastal development, climate-resilient infrastructure, and community housing policy.

Education

STEM workforce pipelines, marine literacy, and public science communication across Maine communities.
