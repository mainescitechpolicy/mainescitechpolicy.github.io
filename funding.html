Pew Charitable Trusts Match-Based Funding Initiative

The Maine State SciTech Policy Fellowship is actively applying for a match-based grant from The Pew Charitable Trusts, supported by funding from the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation and coordinated in partnership with the National Conference of State Legislatures (NCSL).

This national initiative helps states launch nonpartisan science and technology policy fellowship programs by matching local philanthropic and institutional commitments—doubling the impact of every dollar raised in Maine.

How Matching Contributions Work

  1. Local & Philanthropic Commitments: Foundations, university partners, and individual donors pledge support toward fellow stipends, health benefits, and professional development.
  2. National Match Unlock: Verified local commitments count toward our matching requirement for the Pew Charitable Trusts / Moore Foundation grant coordinated with NCSL.
  3. Direct Placement in Maine: 100% of pooled funds support nonpartisan doctoral fellows serving Maine’s legislature and state agencies across coastal science, energy, housing, and education.
